FIRST THINGS FIRST

Get to know your skin.

Your skin has its own needs. Learning about common skin types can help you choose products that feel right for you.

✿
01

Normal Skin

Generally balanced, without excessive oiliness or noticeable dryness.

Care tipKeep to a gentle cleanser, moisturizer, and sunscreen.
✧
02

Oily Skin

Produces more sebum, which can make the face look shiny and may contribute to clogged pores.

Care tipChoose gentle cleansing and lightweight, non-comedogenic products.
❋
03

Dry Skin

May feel tight, rough, or flaky and can benefit from extra moisture.

Care tipTry a gentle cleanser and a richer, fragrance-free moisturizer.
◒
04

Combination Skin

Often has an oily T-zone and cheeks that may feel normal or dry.

Care tipUse products that suit the different needs of each area.
♡
05

Sensitive Skin

May react to certain products with redness, itching, burning, or discomfort.

Care tipLook for gentle, fragrance-free products and patch-test new ones.

Skin types can overlap, and sensitivity can occur with any skin type. These descriptions are general guidance, not a diagnosis.

LESS, BUT BETTER

Your daily routine, made simple.

You don't need a shelf full of products. Start with three basic steps and build a routine that works for your skin.

STEP 01
◌

Cleanse

Remove dirt and excess oil.

A gentle cleanser helps remove sweat, dirt, sunscreen, and excess oil without stripping your skin.

HOW TOUse lukewarm water and your fingertips. Avoid harsh scrubbing.
STEP 02
♡

Moisturize

Support your skin barrier.

Moisturizer helps reduce water loss, soften the skin, and support its natural protective barrier.

HOW TOApply a suitable moisturizer after cleansing, while skin is slightly damp.
STEP 03
☼

Protect

Make sunscreen a daily habit.

Sunscreen helps protect exposed skin from UV radiation, sunburn, premature aging, and skin cancer.

HOW TOUse broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher. Reapply about every two hours outdoors and after swimming or sweating.
✦

Remember: A simple, consistent routine is often better than using too many products at once.

LET'S CLEAR THINGS UP

Skincare myths vs. facts.

There's a lot of skincare advice out there. Let's separate common myths from helpful facts.

× MYTH 01

Oily skin doesn't need moisturizer.

✓ FACT

Oily skin can still benefit from moisturizer. Choose a lightweight, non-comedogenic formula.

× MYTH 02

Sunscreen is only needed on sunny days.

✓ FACT

UV exposure can happen on cloudy days, too. Use broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher when appropriate.

× MYTH 03

More products mean better results.

✓ FACT

Too many products or active ingredients may irritate skin. A simple routine is a good place to start.

× MYTH 04

Popping pimples helps them heal faster.

✓ FACT

Picking or squeezing pimples can worsen inflammation and increase the risk of scarring.

× MYTH 05

Natural products are always safe.

✓ FACT

Natural ingredients can still cause irritation or allergies. Check ingredients and patch-test new products.

× MYTH 06

Scrubbing harder makes skin cleaner.

✓ FACT

Harsh scrubbing can irritate skin. Cleanse gently with your fingertips instead.
